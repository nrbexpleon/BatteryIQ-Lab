# Methods and limitations

- **SOC:** trapezoidal coulomb counting from the first reported SOC. Accuracy depends on current-sensor bias, timing, capacity and initial SOC.
- **Capacity SOH:** discharged ampere-hours divided by observed SOC-window fraction. Short or non-representative windows can be misleading.
- **Resistance SOH:** voltage/current step ratio for steps of at least 5 A. Temperature, SOC, contactors and measurement dynamics affect the estimate.
- **Combined SOH:** simple mean of available capacity and resistance indicators. It is explainable, not an electrochemical truth model.
- **Range:** observed positive traction energy per distance and SOH-adjusted nominal energy. Terrain, climate, speed, payload and auxiliaries can materially change actual range.
- **Thermal/cell diagnostics:** threshold methods detect known deviations but do not prove absence of internal faults.

Production thresholds must be defined by authorized battery and safety engineers using vehicle-specific evidence.
